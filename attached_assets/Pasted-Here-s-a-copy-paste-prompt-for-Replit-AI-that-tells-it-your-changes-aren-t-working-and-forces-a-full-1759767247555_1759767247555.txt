Prompt for Replit AI — Change Audit (no code changes)

Context: Things I report still aren’t fixed. I need an exact audit of everything you changed when you were “fixing bank statements” and “the currency thing”. Since then I’m seeing PWA-only scroll regressions (Processing, Success, Dashboard-after-Back) and bottom nav unlocks.

Do this now (diagnose only):
	1.	Produce a Change Log (past 7–14 days):

	•	Did you change BottomNav placement, positioning, or parent container?

	•	Are Processing/Success using a different shell or transition wrapper than Dashboard?
	•	Is the Service Worker serving an older stylesheet in PWA?
	6.	Verification plan (no code changes):
	•	Provide a step-by-step repro matrix (device, PWA vs browser, route path) and a console snippet that enumerates scrollable ancestors. Include its output per screen.
